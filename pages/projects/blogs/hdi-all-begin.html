How did it all begin?

2026.03.17 · Philosophy

Since the dawn of humanity, one question has persisted: how did it all begin? It is a question that spans science, myth, and imagination.

From stars to cells, from stories to equations, every attempt to answer this question is an invitation to explore the edge of what we know. The first moment is still woven into the unknown.

What we can say

We know the universe expanded from a very hot, dense state. We know atoms, stars, and life emerged over billions of years. But the first trigger remains a deeper mystery.

Why it matters

Asking how it began helps us think better about cause, change, and the limits of explanation. It is not only a scientific question, it is also the story we tell about our place in the universe.
